# ep001 storyboard review (stills round 2, commit 68c9da0)

Contact sheet: https://github.com/NeatKnight18586/inktime/raw/renders/renders/ep001-titanic-key-storyboard/contact.jpg
Two lines per beat. Mute: what a viewer with the sound off understands. Match: does the picture show what the words say.

**b01 L1 "forty"**: the aerial hook, frame 0
- Mute: a lit liner at night seen from far off, a WHOOOSH, a small red clock pointing at it; "forty seconds" in yellow. Something is about to happen to this ship.
- Match: yes. Titanic is on screen as "Titanic" is named; the berg plant is in the foreground dark. WHOOOSH is cut off at the left edge.

**b02 L2 "You're"**: landing in the nest
- Mute: a first-person view from a high lookout over a dark sea, a hand on the rim, a watch-guide with a full ring at the right.
- Match: yes, partly. It reads "in the crow's nest", but Tick is small, not "big".

**b03 L2 "freezing"**: hands rub together, breath wisp
- Mute: two hands rubbing in front of you, breath curls rising; it is cold.
- Match: yes. The heavy end-on cuffs dominate the hands.

**b04 L2 "binoculars"**: hands on the BINOCULARS crate lid
- Mute: looking down at a wooden crate beside you, a hand reaching for it.
- Match: partly. The crate is clear but its "BINOCULARS" stencil faces away from this camera, so the word on screen is only the caption.

**b05 L2 "Locked"**: padlock close-up
- Mute: a brass padlock on a crate stencilled "BINOCULARS"; it is locked shut.
- Match: yes. The lock fills about 30% of the width; the stencil reads as "OCULA" (cropped).

**b06 L3 "look"**: Lee on the rail, not looking at you
- Mute: a sailor in a knit cap, bored, half-lidded.
- Match: no. Lee's face is still turned toward the camera; "not looking at you" does not read.

**b07 L3 "sink"**: Lee turns to you, smug
- Mute: the same sailor smirking at you.
- Match: yes.

**b08 L4 "Thirty"**: Tick close, ring at 30
- Mute: the clock-guide floats in front of you, arms out, its ring part-drained.
- Match: yes. "Thirty" is yellow; the ring shows time running out.

**b09 L4 "Nothing"**: empty pocket lining, a moth
- Mute: looking down at your coat, a pale lump in front of you, a small moth.
- Match: partly. The lining reads as a grey rock, not a pocket; the moth is small but there.

**b10 L5 "flat"**: mirror-flat sea with the stars
- Mute: looking down over the rim at a calm, dashed sea; a crate in the corner.
- Match: partly. The sea is calm, but the mirrored stars don't read at this size, and the rim and crate take half the frame.

**b11 L5 "close"**: the small dark berg, no foam
- Mute: a flat sea with a tiny dark shape on the horizon.
- Match: yes. The speck is easy to miss, as intended.

**b12 L6 "phone"**: phone in your hand, "NO SERVICE"
- Mute: a modern phone in your hand in 1912; the screen says SERVICE.
- Match: partly. The phone reads, but it is cropped so only "SERVICE" shows.

**b13 L6 "twelve"**: Tick slaps the phone; it drops
- Mute: SLAP, an open hand, the clock-guide at your hand, a small phone falling.
- Match: yes. The falling phone is small.

**b14 L7 "Twenty"**: the jump begins
- Mute: the clock-guide jumps to the centre with arms out; "Twenty" in yellow.
- Match: partly. Tick is centred but about 20% wide, not 40%, and the hands' spin and the ink splash aren't visible at this frame.

**b15 L7 "days"**: the dock, the officer at the gangway
- Mute: daylight, a black hull with portholes, a gangway, a crowd, an officer stepping off; stamp "SOUTHAMPTON · APRIL 1912".
- Match: yes. Place and date read without sound.

**b16 L8 "pocket"**: he pats his pocket, the key glints
- Mute: an officer with a moustache, a glint at his coat pocket.
- Match: yes. "pocket" is yellow; the glint marks where the key is.

**b17 L9 "that"**: Fleet faces you, squinting, pointing
- Mute: the lookout in a dark cap squints, mouth open, a hand pointing; Tick next to his face.
- Match: yes. The pointing arm is partly cut off at the frame edge.

**b18 L10 "Iceberg"**: money shot, DING-DING-DING
- Mute: a large pale-topped berg on a flat sea, DING-DING-DING overhead.
- Match: yes. The berg spans about 45% of the width and stays fully in frame.

**b19 L11 "never"**: the auction, the key on velvet
- Mute: a big brass key on red velvet; stamp "AUCTION · 2007"; part of a price card.
- Match: yes. "never a key" plus a key on display: the twist reads.

**b20 L11 "thousand"**: the gavel, SOLD, £90,000
- Mute: a warm auction room, a small auctioneer behind a podium, SOLD, heads in the foreground.
- Match: partly. SOLD reads, but the £90,000 card is out of frame and the gavel slam is too small to read.

**b21 L12 "time"**: back at the aerial, mid-rise
- Mute: the liner from above and behind, moving away.
- Match: yes.
